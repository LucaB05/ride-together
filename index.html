The problem

Riding is better together. Finding company is hard.

Most riders already want someone to ride with — they just have no simple way to find them.

Scattered groups

Rides get planned in closed chats and old forum threads. If you're new in town, you're not in them.

Hard to find a match

Pace, bike and riding style matter. It's hard to know who fits before you've already met.

Location feels risky

Most location apps share too much, for too long. Riders want control over who sees them — and when.

The live map

See who's riding — right now.

Open the app and the map shows riders near you who have chosen to be visible. No old check-ins, no guessing: if they're on the map, they're out riding.

  • Real-time positions

    Riders move on the map as they ride, updated every few seconds.

  • Tap a rider

    See their bike, experience and riding style — then send a ride request or head their way.

  • Filter what you see

    Switch city, show only friends, or narrow it down to riders who ride like you.

  • Always up to date

    When someone stops sharing or loses connection, they disappear from the map within a minute.

How it works

Four simple ways to get riding.

Build a profile with your bike, experience and interests. Then pick the way that suits you.

1

Join a rideout

Browse planned rides and meets near you, join or apply, and coordinate the meeting point.

2

Send a ride request

See a rider that fits? Introduce yourself. Once they accept, you can chat and plan the ride.

3

Meet on the map

When a rider chooses to be visible, head their way and join up — directions open in your maps app.

4

Ride with friends

Message riders you've connected with, drop a meeting pin, and set a time.

Privacy & safety

Hidden by default. You decide the rest.

Ride Together is built so you never share more than you mean to.

Location off until you turn it on

Choose who can see you — friends only, or all signed-in riders — and stop sharing with one tap.

Only live, never history

We don't record your routes. When you stop sharing or go offline, your position disappears.

Extra protection for younger riders

Riders under 18 share location with friends only and can't be contacted first by adult strangers.

Block and report

Blocking someone immediately cuts off contact and location access. Reports go to real moderators.
